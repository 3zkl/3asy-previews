Services

Hair services

Cutting, colouring, styling, extensions and smoothing treatments, as listed on the salon’s hair services page. No public prices were listed.

  • CuttingWet cuts and cut and finish, including curly, bobbed, straight and clippered hair. Pedro, Holly and Debs also offer men’s cuts.
  • ColouringLived-in colour, balayage, grey blending, foiling, glossing and creative colour, using brands the salon names as Goldwell, L’Oréal and Wella. New clients are asked to book a patch test and consultation before a colour appointment.
  • Blow-dries and hair-upBouncy blow-dries, waves and smooth finishes, plus dry styling and hair-up for events, with trials recommended for bigger occasions.
  • Hair extensionsPedro, Holly and Becky offer a variety of methods. A consultation is required.
  • Keratin straighteningOffered by Pedro and Holly; the salon asks you to book a consultation.
  • Children’s hairListed among the salon’s additional hair services.

Lash and brow treatments

The salon says looks are tailored to the client, and recommends a free consultation if you are unsure where to start. No public prices were listed.

  • Classic lash extensionsOne individual lash applied to one natural lash.
  • Hybrid lash extensionsDescribed as a 50/50 mix of classic and volume lashes.
  • Russian volume lash extensionsSeveral fine lashes applied to one natural lash, for a fuller look.
  • Lash lift and tintThe salon says lashes stay tinted and curled for up to six weeks.
  • Brow laminationThe salon says this can last from 4 to 8 weeks, depending on growth. A patch test is required.
  • Brow tint and shapeTinting and shaping, together or separately, with soft aloe strip wax for brow services.

Beauty treatments

Waxing, massage and facials, as listed on the salon’s beauty services page. No public prices were listed.

  • Radiance facialDescribed as a double cleanse, tone, exfoliate, mask, facial massage, serum and moisturiser, with optional steam, extractions and scalp massage.
  • MicroneedlingListed as a facial treatment, with a pen or a roller.
  • DermaplaningListed as a non-invasive facial treatment.
  • MassageFull body, deep tissue, back, neck and shoulders, and Indian head massage.
  • WaxingFacial, intimate and body waxing, using strip and hot wax.

Services are taken from studiothirteensalon.co.uk and have not been confirmed with the salon. No treatment prices were listed on the public site, so none have been added here. A real site would include the current price list, once the salon was happy to share it, alongside booking.

How to book

The salon says the easiest way to book is on Fresha, including complimentary consultations, patch tests, appointments and the waitlist. If the phone isn’t answered, they recommend WhatsApp, as the team may be with clients.
