On the menu

Food and specials

All plant-based. Specials change weekly.

  • Plant-based breakfastsIncluding a plant-based black pudding, which a reviewer said beats the meat version
  • Sandwiches and wrapsChickpea “tuna” sandwich, seitan Caesar wrap
  • SoupsCream of celery with sourdough, creamy mushroom
  • Weekly specialsTeriyaki jackfruit burger, naan-wichs, tempeh and rice bowls, vegan beef stew
  • Frittata, buddha bowls and ramenMentioned on public listings
  • Sausage rolls, muffins and cakesA couple of wheat-free and sugar-free options, per a review

Drinks

  • Coffee“Fab coffee”
