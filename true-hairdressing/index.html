A family salon since 2001

True Hairdressing was started by Phil and Jane Hales, with their daughter Jade part of the team. Phil’s about page notes a British Hairdressing Awards finals place in 1995, and Jade is described as a Wella Master Colour Expert.

From the salon’s About True page.

The salon experience

Set over two floors, with comfortable seating, complimentary Nespresso coffee, teas and spring water, magazines, and music. Colour and care products are described as Wella, including System Professional, plus Nioxin and Sebastian. Allergy testing is taken seriously.
